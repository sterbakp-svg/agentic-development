```text
Job title: Data Protection Officer (DPO)
Organization: a regional network of private hospitals and outpatient clinics
Reports to: Chief Executive Officer, with a direct line to the Supervisory Board

About the role
You are the organization's independent data protection officer. You advise management and staff on personal data processing, monitor compliance and are the contact point for the data protection authority and for patients who exercise their rights.

What you will do
- Monitor compliance with GDPR and the national data protection act across all hospitals and clinics
- Advise on data protection impact assessments (DPIA) for new systems and projects
- Handle data subject requests from patients and employees within the legal deadlines
- Assess personal data breaches with IT Security and prepare notifications to the authority within 72 hours
- Review data processing agreements with vendors of medical and IT systems
- Run privacy training for medical and administrative staff
- Report on data protection to the CEO and the Supervisory Board twice a year

Current priorities
- rollout of a new electronic health record system
- NIS2 readiness together with IT Security

You work closely with IT Security, Legal, Quality and Patient Safety, HR, hospital directors and external vendors.

Requirements
- Degree in law or IT and 3+ years in data protection
- CIPP/E certification is an advantage

How to apply
Send your CV to careers@example.com by the end of the month. Questions: +421 900 000 000.
```
